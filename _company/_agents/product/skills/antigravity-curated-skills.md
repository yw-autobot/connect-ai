# Antigravity Curated Skills

Source repository: https://github.com/sickn33/antigravity-awesome-skills.git

## Primary Matches

- `ai-product` - AI product strategy and product design patterns.
- `product-marketing-context` - ICP, use cases, positioning, and messaging context.
- `micro-saas-launcher` - fast validation and small SaaS launch workflow.
- `pricing-strategy` - pricing and packaging input for product decisions.
- `analytics-product` - product metrics and analytics thinking.

## Operating Rule

Product should convert upstream guidance into PRDs, acceptance criteria, and small experiments. It should not expand scope without CEO alignment.
