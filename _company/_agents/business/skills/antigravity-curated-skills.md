# Antigravity Curated Skills

Use these upstream skills as reference patterns when Business is working on revenue, pricing, go-to-market, product validation, or CRM operations.

Source repository: https://github.com/sickn33/antigravity-awesome-skills.git

## Primary Matches

- `revops` - lead lifecycle, routing, handoffs, CRM process, and revenue operations.
- `micro-saas-launcher` - indie-style small SaaS validation, launch, and iteration.
- `pricing-strategy` - pricing, packaging, monetization, and value-based willingness-to-pay reasoning.
- `business-analyst` - business analysis, metrics, dashboards, and data-driven recommendations.
- `marketing-ideas` - prioritized growth ideas for SaaS and software products.
- `product-marketing-context` - positioning, ICP, messaging, and reusable product marketing context.

## Operating Rule

Business should convert these skills into measurable experiments, pricing hypotheses, and CEO-ready recommendations.
